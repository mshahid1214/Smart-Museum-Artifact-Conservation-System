Task 1: Identify Operations
Smart Museum Artifact Conservation System

The following operations are identified from the scenario.

No.	Operation	Description
1	performSelfCheck()	Checks sensors and environmental-control devices.
2	validateSensorStatus()	Verifies that essential sensors are working correctly.
3	registerArtifact()	Records the artifact identification information.
4	loadEnvironmentalProfile()	Loads the artifact's required environmental limits.
5	monitorArtifact()	Continuously monitors the artifact and chamber.
6	checkDoorStatus()	Checks whether the chamber door is open or closed.
7	activateConservation()	Starts normal conservation activities.
8	measureTemperature()	Reads the current chamber temperature.
9	measureHumidity()	Reads the current chamber humidity.
10	compareEnvironmentalLimits()	Compares actual conditions with required limits.
11	correctTemperature()	Attempts to restore temperature to the permitted range.
12	correctHumidity()	Attempts to restore humidity to the permitted range.
13	verifyEnvironmentalRecovery()	Verifies that environmental correction was successful.
14	startRecoveryTimer()	Tracks the permitted recovery period.
15	activateProtectionControls()	Activates additional protection measures.
16	generateOperatorAlert()	Sends an alert to the museum operator.
17	detectVibration()	Detects significant vibration around the artifact.
18	suspendRiskyActivities()	Suspends activities that could increase artifact risk.
19	verifyVibrationStabilization()	Verifies that vibration remains below the permitted threshold.
20	suspendConservation()	Suspends normal conservation activities.
21	verifySafeResumption()	Verifies conditions before conservation resumes.
22	switchToEmergencyPower()	Switches the system to emergency power.
23	recordPowerFailure()	Records a power failure incident.
24	shutdownSafely()	Places the system into a safe shutdown condition.
25	verifyArtifactRemovalSafety()	Checks whether artifact removal is safe.
26	authorizeArtifactRemoval()	Authorizes artifact removal after safety verification.
